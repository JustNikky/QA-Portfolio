## TC-PRODUCT-011 - Product can be opened from product name or image
---

| **Field** | **Value** |
|---|---|
| **Test case ID** | TC-PRODUCT-011 |
| **Test type** | Functional |
| **Title** | Product can be opened from product name or image |
| **Module** | Product |
| **Priority** | Medium |
| **Preconditions** | User is on the SauceDemo Products page after successful login. |
| **Test data** | `Sauce Labs Backpack` |
| **Steps** | 1. Locate the `Sauce Labs Backpack` product.<br><br>2. Click the product name.<br><br>3. Verify that the product details page for `Sauce Labs Backpack` is displayed.<br><br>4. Navigate back to the Products page.<br><br>5. Click the product image for `Sauce Labs Backpack`.<br><br>6. Verify that the product details page for `Sauce Labs Backpack` is displayed.  |
| **Expected result** |  Clicking the product name or product image opens the corresponding product details page for `Sauce Labs Backpack`. |
| **Actual result** | Clicking the product name opened the `Sauce Labs Backpack` product details page. Clicking the product image also opened the `Sauce Labs Backpack` product details page. |
| **Status** | **PASS** |
| **Defect** | N/A |
